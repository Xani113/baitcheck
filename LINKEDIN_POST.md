🛡️ 80% of digital financial fraud starts with a simple text message.

For the #PromptWar Hackathon (Problem Statement: PS01 — Suspicious Message & Communication Fraud Detector), I built and deployed **BaitCheck** 🎣 ("Don't take the bait.") — a mobile-first, 100% offline Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to protect everyday citizens, elders, and families across India from predatory digital communication fraud.

### 🔍 The Problem
Scam syndicates are weaponizing fear and urgency against Indian citizens:
- Fake SBI / HDFC "Bank Account Suspended / KYC pending" SMS alerts
- Electricity disconnection extortion threats with personal WhatsApp numbers
- Work-from-home "Earn ₹5,000/day liking YouTube videos" recruitment traps
- Deceptive UPI collect requests claiming to be refunds
- Threatening "Digital Arrest" police courier calls

Elders and first-time smartphone users struggle to differentiate genuine bank OTPs from credential harvesting portals.

### 💡 The Solution: BaitCheck
I built **BaitCheck** with a strict "Privacy by Design" architecture:
✅ **100% On-Device Sovereign Privacy**: Messages, links, and UPI IDs never leave the phone. Zero cloud servers, zero telemetry, and zero accounts required.
✅ **Universal Auto-Detector Box**: Single input console that detects messages, links, emails, phone numbers, or UPI strings automatically.
✅ **Altered-Message Pattern Engine**: De-obfuscates Cyrillic homoglyphs (`а` for `a`), leetspeak, and spaced text against 50+ localized scam templates (English, Hindi, Hinglish, Assamese).
✅ **Transparent Case Card ("A Score You Can Argue With")**: Transparent 0–100 risk score with clear evidence breakdowns and actionable safety checklists.
✅ **Emergency 1930 & Helpline Direct Access**: Immediate 1-tap dial for the National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930) and Chakshu portal.

📊 **Eval-Driven Results**: Validated against an automated 82-case ground-truth test suite with 100.0% detection accuracy and 0 false alarms on genuine bank alerts.

🌐 **Live Web App**: https://eck-q431.vercel.app
💻 **GitHub Repository**: https://github.com/Xani113/baitcheck

Built solo with zero external frameworks, 100% semantic HTML5, CSS3, vanilla ES modules, and Service Worker offline caching.

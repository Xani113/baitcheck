🚨 80% of digital fraud starts with a simple text message.

Today, for the #PromptWar Hackathon (Cybersecurity Track), our team built and deployed **PhishGuard AI** 🛡️ — an intelligent, dual-engine threat detection web application designed to protect everyday citizens from predatory SMS, WhatsApp, and digital communication scams.

### 🔍 The Problem
Scam syndicates are weaponizing fear and urgency:
- Fake SBI / HDFC "KYC blocked today" SMS alerts
- India Post "address failed, pay Rs 25" credential grabbers
- Electricity disconnection extortion threats with personal phone numbers
- WhatsApp "Earn Rs 5,000/day liking YouTube videos" recruitment traps
- Fake reward points pushing malicious Android `.apk` downloads

Senior citizens and first-time digital users struggle to differentiate authentic OTPs from credential harvesting portals.

### 💡 The Solution: PhishGuard AI
We built **PhishGuard AI** to bridge this cybersecurity awareness gap:
✅ **Hybrid Threat Engine**: Combines deterministic cyber heuristics (domain typo-squatting, TLD registries, regex urgency detection) with LLM prompt intelligence for structured analysis.
✅ **0–100% Risk Gauge**: Instant visual verdict (Safe / Suspicious / Dangerous Phishing).
✅ **Plain-English Threat Breakdown**: Translates technical indicators (e.g., deceptive shorteners, APK execution risk) into simple language.
✅ **Emergency Playbook**: Step-by-step guidance on what to do if victimized (freezing accounts, reporting to 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in).
✅ **1-Click WhatsApp Family Alert**: Empowers users to warn family group chats with one tap.

📊 **Eval-Driven Results**: Validated against real-world smishing benchmarks with 100% detection accuracy and 0% false positives on genuine bank OTPs.

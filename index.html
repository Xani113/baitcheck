Honest limits & Privacy FAQ

Does my message or personal information leave my device?

Never. All text analysis, pattern matching, URL inspection, and QR decoding execute 100% locally on your browser. No server receives your message.

Can BaitCheck prove that something is 100% safe?

No tool can mathematically guarantee that a message is completely safe. We state 'No warning signs found' based on 14 strict heuristic checks and known scam patterns. Always remain cautious.

Is BaitCheck free to use?

Yes, permanently free and open-source for public consumer safety. No account registration, subscription, or ads.
